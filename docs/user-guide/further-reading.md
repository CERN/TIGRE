# Further reading

**The TIGRE papers.** The original paper describes the toolbox, its
geometry and its algorithms; the multi-GPU paper explains how TIGRE splits
large problems; the TIGRE v3 paper covers the current version and its use on
real datasets. Full references and BibTeX are on [](../citing.md).

- [TIGRE: a MATLAB-GPU toolbox for CBCT image reconstruction](https://doi.org/10.1088/2057-1976/2/5/055010)
  (Biomedical Physics & Engineering Express, 2016, open access)
- [Arbitrarily large iterative tomographic reconstruction on multiple GPUs using the TIGRE toolbox](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpdc.2020.07.004)
  (Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing, 2020;
  [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03748))
- [TIGRE v3: Efficient and easy to use iterative computed tomographic reconstruction toolbox for real datasets](https://doi.org/10.1088/2631-8695/adbb3a)
  (Engineering Research Express, 2025)

**Iterative reconstruction, GPUs and TIGRE in depth.** Ander Biguri's PhD
thesis, *Iterative Reconstruction and Motion Compensation in Computed
Tomography on GPUs* (University of Bath, 2018), explains the algorithms and
their GPU implementation in detail.
[Thesis and source](https://github.com/AnderBiguri/PhDThesis).

**Motion compensation.** [A general method for motion compensation in X-ray
computed tomography](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6560/aa7675) (Physics in
Medicine and Biology, 2017, open access), with code at
[MotionTIGRE](https://github.com/AnderBiguri/MotionTIGRE).

**Research that uses TIGRE.** See [](../papers.md) for more than 370 papers,
grouped by field.
